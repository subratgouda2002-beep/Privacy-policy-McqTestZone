Welcome to MCQ Test Zone. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our services.

MCQ Test Zone provides online practice tests and educational resources for students preparing for central and state competitive examinations.

1. Information We Collect

MCQ Test Zone does not require users to create an account or register to access its main features.

We do not directly request your name, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone through the application.

However, third-party services integrated into the app, including advertising services, may automatically process certain technical and device-related information.

2. Local Progress and Score Storage

The application provides features such as Attempted Tests, Recent Attempts and Score History.

To support these features, quiz attempt records are stored locally on your device using Android Room SQLite database and DataStore Preferences.

Your Privacy: Quiz progress stored in the application's local database remains on your device and is not transmitted by the app's progress-tracking feature to our servers.

You can delete locally stored progress by using the Reset Score option in the Profile/Score section or by uninstalling the application.

3. In-App WebView and External Quiz Content

MCQ Test Zone provides access to online practice tests through an in-app browser (WebView).

The application loads educational content from:

https://www.mcqtestzone.in/

When you visit external quiz pages, the website may process information such as cookies, temporary browser data, analytics information and other technical information according to its own privacy practices.

4. Google AdMob and Advertising

MCQ Test Zone uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and help support the free availability of educational content.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID).
  • Device information and operating system details.
  • Approximate location information derived from IP address.
  • Advertising impressions and interactions.
  • Diagnostic and advertising performance data.

These technologies may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and other advertising-related purposes.

Google Privacy Resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information

You can manage available advertising personalization and Advertising ID options through your Android device's privacy settings.

5. Application Permissions

MCQ Test Zone uses the following permissions for its functionality and integrated services:

  • INTERNET: Allows the application to load online educational content and advertisements.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the application to check network connectivity.
  • AD_ID: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising-related services.

6. Sharing Features

The application may provide a Share feature that allows you to share an invitation link through Android's native sharing interface.

You choose which application and recipient receive the shared information. MCQ Test Zone does not read or store your contact list through this feature.

7. Data Security

We use HTTPS connections for supported online services to help protect information transferred over the internet.

Locally stored quiz progress is kept within the application's private storage on your device.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

MCQ Test Zone is designed for competitive examination aspirants and general audiences.

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 years of age.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through a linked webpage, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services, including Google AdMob and external educational websites.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and may process information according to their respective terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in application functionality, third-party services or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
